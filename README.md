### 💮 About Me :

I'm Leah, a fourth-year student studying CS and philosophy at Barnard College, Columbia University. <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/dCjgjlXrflbRFqNiRL/giphy.gif?cid=790b7611f2jk908lmayrilq5m5v20ca2k58uvpjycc4v8i3c&ep=v1_stickers_search&rid=giphy.gif&ct=s" width="30"> 

My interests are in everything related to the visual and the experience. Things that require craft and good judgement. This preference makes me interested in experience design, frontend engineering, and immersive + emerging technologies. I like to be a problem solver first, as I value pinpointing and understanding the problem correctly first before diving into the code. 

My favorite way to do this is by talking with the people first-- the user, or client, or whomever-- and becoming one of them too, so I can comprehend where the challenges and solutions lie. I did this a lot during my internship at NASA, where I frequently sat down face-to-face with the (aeroacoustic, not tech) engineers and researchers to figure out how to build the right research tool for them. I toured all of their workspaces, saw how they ran their research, all the data they were dealing with, and identified where things could be made easier. And I built that solution! (PSSTT, it's still being used 1 year after my internship concluded) 

This passion comes from my lovely experience of being a barista during my undergrad. Being a barista is not just about serving good coffee. It's also about dealing with pressure (the 8AM rush is very scary...), working as a team, staying organized, and sticking through it all. It's about showing up at 5AM when no one else is awake, and multi-tasking to continue conversations with regular customers (my fav part!). It's about consistently striving to improve and experimenting with the community :)) 

In this way, I understand what it takes to deliver real value to customers. To be immersed with the target audience, to find real problems, and then sealing those gaps.
